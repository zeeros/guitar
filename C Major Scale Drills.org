* Single String

Play the C Major scale on a single string from open to 12th fret and back.
Mind the zero-fret gaps between E-F and B-C.
Repeat for all strings.

#+name: c-major-scale
#+caption: The C Major scale
| Note | Semitones | Interval | Degree - Name    |
|------+-----------+----------+------------------|
| C    |         0 | —        | 1 - Tonic        |
| D    |         2 | Whole    | 2 - Supertonic   |
| E    |         4 | Whole    | 3 - Mediant      |
| F    |         5 | Half     | 4 - Subdominant  |
| G    |         7 | Whole    | 5 - Dominant     |
| A    |         9 | Whole    | 6 - Submediant   |
| B    |        11 | Whole    | 7 - Leading tone |
| C    |        12 | Half     | 8 - Octave       |

* String Jumps

Play C on all the strings.

#+name: c-frets
#+caption: C on the guitar fretboard within the first 12 frets
| E | A |  D | G | B | E |
|---+---+----+---+---+---|
| 8 | 3 | 10 | 5 | 1 | 8 |

On the same fret where you play C, play F (the 4th) on the string below and G (the 5th) on the string above.
Mind the B-string offset.

#+name: standard-tuning-intervals
#+caption: Intervals between adjacent strings on a standard-tuned guitar
| String     | E   | A   | D   | G   | B   | E   |
|------------+-----+-----+-----+-----+-----+-----|
| Ascending  |     | 4th | 4th | 4th | 3rd | 4th |
| Descending | 5th | 5th | 5th | 6th | 5th |     |
