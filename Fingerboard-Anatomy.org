* Fingerboard Navigation

In standard tuning, most adjacent string pairs are tuned a Perfect 4th (P4) apart, with one exception: G–B, which is a Major 3rd (M3).
This is intentional — it's what makes standard scales and basic chords playable across all keys.
As a consequence, the fret distance required to play the same interval shifts depending on which string pair you're on.
With unison (P1) and octave (P8) as landmarks, any other interval is just a small offset away — whichever anchor is closest on the neck.
